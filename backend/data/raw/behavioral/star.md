# STAR Method

## Structure
- Situation: Context and background.
- Task: Your exact responsibility and goal.
- Action: Specific technical and interpersonal steps YOU personally took.
- Result: Quantifiable or observable outcome.

## Interview Guidance
Keep the story focused on your contribution. Prefer concrete actions and measurable results where available.

## Common Questions
- Tell me about yourself.
- Tell me about a difficult problem you solved.
- Tell me about a time you disagreed with a teammate.
- Tell me about a failure and what you learned.
