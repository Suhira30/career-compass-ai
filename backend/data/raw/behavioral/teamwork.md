# Teamwork Interviews

## Focus Areas
- communication
- collaboration
- ownership
- conflict resolution
- helping teammates
- receiving feedback

## Recommended Structure
Use STAR and clearly distinguish your own contribution from the team's overall work.

## Practice Questions
- Tell me about a successful team project.
- Tell me about a time a teammate needed help.
- How do you handle disagreements in a team?
